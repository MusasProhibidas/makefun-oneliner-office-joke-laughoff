One-line office joke entry for the [One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73) bounty on make.fun ($3).

The entry is a single-line office joke about "per my last email" — the passive-aggressive corporate classic, delivered in one line. The joke lives in `line.txt`; `main.py` validates it (exactly one non-empty line, at most 200 characters) and writes it, without the trailing newline, to the output file.
